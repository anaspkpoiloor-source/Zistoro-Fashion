Return & Replacement Policy

At ZISTORO, each order is checked before dispatch. If there is an issue with your order, please contact us so we can review it.

Change of Mind or Size Preference

At this time, we do not accept returns for change of mind, styling preference, or size selection.

Please review the product details and size guide carefully before placing your order.

This policy does not affect applicable consumer rights regarding products that are damaged, defective, incorrect, or materially different from what was ordered.

Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery.

To help us review your request, please share:

  • Your order number
  • Clear photographs of the product and packaging
  • A continuous, unedited unboxing video showing the sealed parcel being opened, where available

Review & Resolution

Once we receive your details, our team will review the issue and contact you regarding the next steps.

Depending on the verified issue, applicable requirements, and product availability, we may arrange an eligible replacement or refund. The resolution will be communicated to you after review.

Return Shipping

If an issue is verified as our responsibility, ZISTORO will communicate the return or replacement shipping arrangements to you.

Please do not send an item back before receiving return instructions from our team.

Important

Please keep the product, tags, and original packaging until your request has been reviewed.
