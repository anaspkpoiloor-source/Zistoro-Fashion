Shipping & Delivery Policy

At ZISTORO, we aim to process and deliver your order with care. Please review the following information before placing your order.

Delivery Locations

We currently deliver to serviceable PIN codes across India. Delivery availability will be confirmed before your order is finalized.

Order Processing & Dispatch

Orders are processed after order and payment confirmation. Please allow 1–3 working days for dispatch.

If additional processing time is required, we will communicate the update to you.

Estimated Delivery

The estimated delivery time is 4–8 working days after dispatch.

This is an estimate, not a guaranteed delivery date. Actual delivery may vary depending on your location, courier operations, weather, local conditions, or other circumstances beyond our control.

Shipping Charges

Shipping charges will be confirmed with you on WhatsApp before your order is finalized. We will communicate the applicable shipping charge before confirming the order.

Order Tracking

When tracking details are available, we will share them with you through WhatsApp or your provided contact details.

Incorrect Address or Failed Delivery

Please provide a complete and accurate delivery address and contact number.

If a parcel is returned due to an incorrect address, an unavailable recipient, or repeated failed delivery attempts, additional shipping charges may apply for re-dispatch. Any applicable charge must be communicated before re-dispatch.
